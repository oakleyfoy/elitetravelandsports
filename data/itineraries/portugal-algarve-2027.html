Day 1: Arrival at Faro

  • Arrival at Faro airport.
  • Transfer to Hilton Vilamoura As Cascatas Golf Resort & Spa.
  • Check-in and settle in.
  • Welcome dinner at the hotel.

Day 2: Tennis and Faro Old Town

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Transfer to The Campus Tennis Club.
  • Lunch at the club.
  • Explore Faro Old Town, including the cobbled streets, Arco da Vila, the Cathedral, and Bone Chapel.
  • Dinner at Noelia Restaurant.

Day 3: Tennis & Sunset Catamaran Cruise

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Transfer to The Campus Tennis Club.
  • Sunset catamaran cruise from Vilamoura Marina along the coastline with caves and cliffs.
  • Swim stop, lunch, drinks, and music included.
  • Dinner at Tribilum Restaurant.

Day 4: Tennis & Benagil Cave

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Transfer to The Campus Tennis Club.
  • Lunch at Marufo.
  • Benagil Cave and Golden Coastline excursion with kayak, SUP, or boat tour options.
  • Swimming at Praia da Marinha and dinner at Alambique Restaurant.

Day 5: Tennis & Padel Discovery

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Transfer to Vilamoura Tennis & Padel Academy.
  • Lunch at O Ribeiro.
  • One-hour introductory padel session with coach.
  • Dinner at Florian Restaurant.

Day 6: Tennis & Wine Tasting

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Transfer to Vilamoura Tennis & Padel Academy.
  • Lunch during the excursion and wine tasting at Quinta dos Vales near Lagoa.
  • Guided tour with tastings and pairings.
  • Dinner at Alquimia Restaurant.

Day 7: Tennis & Albufeira Evening

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Transfer to Vilamoura Tennis & Padel Academy.
  • Lunch at the clubhouse.
  • Evening in Albufeira, including Old Town, live music, seafood restaurants, and The Strip.
  • Farewell dinner at the hotel.

Day 8: Departure

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Check-out and transfer to Faro Airport.
  • Departure assistance.

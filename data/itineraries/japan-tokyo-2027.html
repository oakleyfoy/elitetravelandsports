Day 1: Arrival in Tokyo

  • Arrival at HND or NRT International Airport.
  • Meet an English-speaking local tour guide at the airport.
  • Check in at Hotel New Otani Tokyo, The Main, the 5-star hotel. Garden Tower, the other Hotel New Otani, is not part of this stay.
  • All nights are in spacious 45 m² Deluxe Rooms at The Main.
  • Welcome dinner at Momo Paradise.

Day 2: Tennis & Culture

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Morning tennis session on the hotel courts.
  • Lunch at a ramen restaurant.
  • TeamLab Planets in Toyosu.
  • Odaiba, including the Statue of Liberty, Gundam, and the shopping marina.
  • Dinner at Yakiniku Rokkasen in Shinjuku.

Day 3: Tennis and Asakusa Tour

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Morning tennis session on the hotel courts.
  • Lunch in Asakusa.
  • Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise Street, and a kimono wearing experience.
  • Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building.
  • Dinner at a geisha show restaurant in Tokyo.

Day 4: Tennis & Art

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Morning tennis session on the hotel courts.
  • Lunch at a gyukatsu restaurant.
  • Shibuya Crossing, the Hachiko statue, and shopping.
  • Meiji Shrine and Akihabara.
  • Dinner at a sushi restaurant.

Day 5: Tennis & UNESCO Mt. Fuji

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Morning tennis session on the hotel courts.
  • Departure to Mt. Fuji.
  • Japanese bento box lunch on the way.
  • Hakone Ropeway to the volcanic area of Owakudani.
  • Scenic cruise on Lake Ashi.
  • Dinner at a tempura restaurant.

Day 6: Tennis & Minato Mirai 21

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Morning tennis session at a private local club.
  • Lunch at a Japanese izakaya.
  • Yokohama Red Brick Warehouse, a stroll at Minato Mirai 21, and Yokohama Chinatown.
  • Dinner at the Hirakuza Sumo Show.

Day 7: Tennis & Hot Spring

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Morning tennis session at a private local club.
  • Lunch during a hot spring experience at a ryokan.
  • Farewell dinner at View & Dining THE SKY at Hotel New Otani Tokyo, The Main, with panoramic views of the city.

Day 8: Departure

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Check-out.
  • Private transfer to HND or NRT International Airport.

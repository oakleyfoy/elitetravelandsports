Morocco is offered on two scheduled departures with set dates and limited availability—the Marrakech Discovery & Tennis Experience itinerary for each. Review the departure, pricing, and day-by-day program, then contact Elite to confirm remaining space.

Morocco

Marrakech Discovery & Tennis Experience

December 9–16, 2026

$5,800 USD per person

Marrakech-centered tennis discovery with daily tennis, guided cultural visits, dining, and transfers.

View full itinerary

Day 1: Arrival, hotel check-in & Moroccan dinner experience

  • Pick up at Marrakech-Menara airport.
  • Transfer to the hotel.
  • Check-in and optional city tour.
  • Moroccan traditional dinner show.

Day 2: Tennis & Marrakech old medina visit

  • Breakfast buffet at the hotel.
  • Morning tennis session with pros, 2 hours.
  • Lunch at a restaurant facing the iconic Koutoubia Mosque.
  • Historical highlights and souks visit with an English-speaking tour guide.
  • Rooftop restaurant dinner show.

Day 3: Tennis, Majorelle Gardens, YSL Museum & Berber Museum

  • Breakfast buffet at the hotel.
  • Morning tennis session with pros, 2 hours.
  • Lunch on a rooftop facing the Majorelle Gardens.
  • Jardins Majorelle, YSL, and Berber Museum visit with a private English-speaking guide.
  • Dinner show in a restaurant built in the 16th century on the site of a former sugar factory.

Day 4: Tennis & excursion to the 3 Berber Valleys of the Atlas Mountains

  • Breakfast buffet at the hotel.
  • Morning tennis session with private coach, 2 hours.
  • Lunch at the tennis clubhouse.
  • Excursion to the 3 Berber Valleys of the Atlas Mountains with an authentic tea experience in a Berber home.
  • International dinner show.

Day 5: Tennis & dinner under the stars in the Agafay Desert

  • Breakfast buffet at the hotel.
  • Morning tennis session with pros, 2 hours.
  • Lunch at the golf clubhouse.
  • Agafay Desert and camel ride in breathtaking landscapes, with optional quad ride.
  • Dinner under the stars in the Agafay camp.

Day 6: Tennis & traditional hammam experience

  • Breakfast buffet at the hotel.
  • Morning tennis session with pros, 2 hours.
  • Lunch at the hotel pool restaurant.
  • Traditional hammam with black soap and henna, exfoliation, ghassoul body treatment, and hydration.
  • Oriental dinner show.

Day 7: Essaouira excursion (Mogador) & farewell dinner

  • Breakfast buffet at the hotel.
  • Excursion to Essaouira for Atlantic views, medina atmosphere, fresh seafood, and a relaxed coastal setting.
  • Oceanfront lunch on site.
  • Farewell dinner at the Lola Sky rooftop.

Day 8: Departure

  • Breakfast buffet at the hotel.
  • Morning at leisure depending on flight time.
  • Airport transfer and departure assistance.

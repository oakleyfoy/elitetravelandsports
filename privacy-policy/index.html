Privacy Policy

How Elite Travel collects information from this website and how that information is used.

Last updated September 29, 2026.

This Privacy Policy describes the personal information collected by Elite Travel, the brand name of Elite Travel & Sports Tours USA, operated by War Experiences LLC. It applies to inquiries submitted on elitetravelsportsusa.com and to text messages sent by Elite Travel.

Information Elite Travel collects

When you submit an inquiry, Elite Travel may collect:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your mobile phone number, if you provide one.
  • Trip details you choose to share, such as preferred sport, travel type, destination, group size, travel window, lodging style, a selected departure, and notes.
  • Whether you checked the box agreeing to receive text messages from Elite Travel.
  • A spam-protection token from Google reCAPTCHA, used only to screen the form.

How Elite Travel uses information

Elite Travel uses this information to:

  • Read and reply to your inquiry by email.
  • Plan a tennis journey and follow up about availability, timing, and next steps.
  • Send text messages from Elite Travel when you have opted in by checking the consent box and providing your mobile number.
  • Protect the inquiry forms from spam and abuse.

Checking the text-message box is optional. Elite Travel can still respond to an inquiry if you do not opt in to texts. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

SMS opt-in data

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Elite Travel uses service providers to host this website, deliver inquiry email, and send the text messages you requested. Those providers may process your information only to perform those services for Elite Travel. They may not use your SMS opt-in data or personal information for their own marketing.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of text messages from Elite Travel. See the SMS Terms for the full program terms.

Contact Elite Travel

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to info@elitetravelsportsusa.com.
